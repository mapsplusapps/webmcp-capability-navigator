# Hackathon provenance

Capability Navigator's WebMCP product layer was built during the WebMCP Challenge submission period, August 25–September 3, 2026.

Pre-existing Maps + Apps material is limited to public portfolio/capability evidence about prior work. The challenge-period implementation includes the typed `document.modelContext.registerTool(...)` browser contract, six bounded tools, deterministic capability matching and packet preparation, visible Agent Trace, same-page human-review staging, ordinary-browser fallback, origin-isolation deployment headers, the demo/evaluation path, and release verification.

The public repository commit history timestamps the challenge-period source additions. The evidence records remain claims about prior Maps + Apps work; the WebMCP interaction layer was new work created during that period.

## Competition status

The project itself was completed during the challenge period, but the final Devpost competition submission was not completed before the deadline. Devpost subsequently declined a late-submission request. As a result, Capability Navigator was **not included in challenge judging and does not claim an award, placement, finalist status, or judged submission**.

The public Devpost project page remains available as a portfolio/demo artifact:

https://devpost.com/software/capability-navigator-give-browser-agents-tools-not-pixels

Challenge-era files such as `DEVPOST_SUBMISSION.md`, `JUDGE_PROMPT.md`, `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json`, and the recording workflow are retained for provenance and auditability even where their filenames reflect the original competition context.
